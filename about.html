About Us

Who we are, how the listings work, and how to get in touch.

Who we are

We are a small, independent team that puts together planning listings for group trips. We gather things to do, places to eat, and places to stay, then organize them so a whole weekend can be planned in one place.

Every listing is labeled with the town it is in, and things to do, nightlife, and charters sit together in one collection so everything is easy to scan at a glance.
