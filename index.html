Bachelorette activities, nightlife, and boat charters

Everything in one place, ordered from the quiet south end toward the lights. Each card is labeled with its town, or Whole Grand Strand if it covers all of them.

Price ranges

Each card shows a typical price per person: Free, $ (under $25), $$ ($25 to $60), $$$ ($60 to $120), and $$$$ (over $120). These are estimates that change with the season, so confirm current prices with each business.

Express Watersports

Whole Grand Strand Watersports and charters

Price range: $–$$$$ About $20 to $150 per person, depending on the activity. Pontoon rentals are priced per boat.

Parasailing, jet skis, dolphin cruises, sunset cruises, banana boat rides, catamaran cruises, and kayak, paddleboard, and pontoon rentals. Family-owned since 1997 with USCG-certified captains, based on the MarshWalk in Murrells Inlet, and runs trips for guests staying up and down the Grand Strand.

Hammock Shops Village

Pawleys Island Shopping

Price range: Free Free to browse. Shopping and food cost extra.

Boutiques, galleries, and cafes tucked under live oaks. A relaxed first afternoon before the weekend gets loud.

Pawleys Island Beach and Historic District

Pawleys Island Beach day

Price range: Free Free to visit.

One of the oldest seaside resorts in America, and locals call the island's style arrogantly shabby. Quiet sand, marsh views, and room to lay out for a slow beach morning.

Brookgreen Gardens

Pawleys Island Gardens

Price range: $$ About $25 per adult, good for 7 consecutive days.

Sculpture gardens and live oak paths just north of the island. A photo-heavy morning and the calmest hour of the trip.

A half-mile creekside boardwalk lined with seafood restaurants and live music most nights. Sunset here is the main event of the weekend.

Group sunset cruises out of the inlet. Bring your own drinks and food, ice is onboard, and guests 21 and up need a photo ID. Boats hold groups of up to about 20.

Plan your rides

Pawleys evenings are quiet, so head to Murrells Inlet for dancing and live bands. Line up a rideshare or a designated driver before the first round, and check each town's alcohol and glass rules before you pack a cooler for the beach.

Book boats early

Sunset cruises, parasailing, and private charters fill up first on summer weekends. Confirm group size limits and the alcohol policy when you book.

Best time to visit

Pick the season first, then build the weekend around it.

Best seasons for a Grand Strand bachelorette weekend
Season Typical months What to expect Best for
Spring March to May Comfortable days and cooler evenings. The ocean is still chilly early on, and crowds are lighter. Brunch crawls, gardens, shopping, patio evenings
Early summer Late May to June Warm water, boats running full schedules, and the first big weekend crowds. Beach days, parasailing, sunset cruises
Peak summer July to August Hot, humid, busiest, and priciest. Afternoon storms are common. Pool-and-beach weekends. Book everything early.
Fall September to October Warm water lingers and crowds thin, which makes it a sweet spot for boat days. Dolphin cruises, MarshWalk nights, easier reservations
Winter November to February Cool and quiet. Some seasonal businesses cut back and the MarshWalk is calmer. Low-key brunch and house-based weekends. Skip the boat day.

Book early

Memorial Day through Labor Day weekends fill beach houses and boat cruises first. Reserve the house and the boat before you plan the rest.

Hurricane season

It runs June 1 through November 30. Choose flexible cancellation terms and check the forecast the week before you go.

Best brunch and restaurants

Brunch tables and group-friendly dinners in every town, with hours worth confirming before you go.

Pawleys Island

Weekend brunch and easy seafood dinners

  • Rustic Table Brunch

    A Pawleys brunch favorite on Ocean Highway with omelets, chicken and waffles, and mimosas.

  • Applewood House of Pancakes Breakfast

    Big breakfast plates and pancakes for a full group on a slow morning.

  • Eggs Up Grill Breakfast

    Reliable breakfast and brunch served until early afternoon.

  • Frank's Outback Seafood dinner

    Seafood in a relaxed setting for the first-night group dinner.

  • Chive Blossom Cafe Local ingredients

    Fresh dishes built around local sourcing.

Murrells Inlet

Waterfront seafood and Sunday brunch

  • Gulfstream Cafe Sunset views

    Deck views over the water, seafood classics, and a Sunday brunch.

  • Dead Dog Saloon Breakfast and brunch

    Breakfast tacos, shrimp and grits, and lobster home fries before the live music starts.

  • Fire to Table Sunday brunch

    A second-floor restaurant with creative sweet and savory brunch dishes on Sundays.

  • Wicked Tuna Seafood dinner

    Dockside seafood with fish brought in by local boats, plus sushi rolls.

  • The Claw House Raw bar

    Oysters, crab legs, and about 90 beers on tap in the Inlet Beer Garden.

  • Creek Ratz Casual seafood

    Fried shrimp, burgers, pizza, and a raw bar right on the MarshWalk.

Surfside Beach

Pier Village dining steps from the sand

  • Gracious Pig Smokehouse Brunch and BBQ

    Indulgent breakfast and brunch plates alongside the smokehouse barbecue.

  • River City Cafe Burgers

    Messy burgers with a view toward the ocean.

  • Pizza Hyena Pizza

  • Chimichanga Llama Mexican-inspired

    A colorful, fresh take on Mexican-inspired favorites.

  • Bubba's Fish Shack Seafood

    Casual seafood in Surfside's entertainment district.

  • Benjamin's Bakery Coffee and pastries

    Beachside pastries, coffee, and bagel breakfast sandwiches to go.

Myrtle Beach

More brunch options up the coast
